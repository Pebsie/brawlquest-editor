The sprites in the group folders here (Aberrations, Beasts, Demons and
Devils, Dragons, Giants and Golems, Humanoids, Insects and Spiders, Plants
and Fungi, Reptiles, Slimes and Oozes, Spirits and Elementals, Undead) are
cut by tools/build_monster_sprites.py from the "Tome" tileset by David E.
Gervais (CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/,
http://pousse.rapiere.free.fr/tome/), already credited on the login screen:
AngbandTk dg_monster1-7, dg_dragon, dg_undead and dg_uniques (32x32), DO
Monsters (54x54) and Silmar monsters. tools/monster_sprites_sources.json
lists the tile each sprite comes from.

Changes: the background is made transparent the same way as the older
monster art, the 54x54 DO and odd-sized Silmar tiles are set bottom-centre
on a 32x32 cell (trailing rows of ground shadow dropped), and the few
creatures bigger than 32 px go on a 64x64 or 96x96 cell like the existing
bosses (file names ending "(2x2)" / "(3x3)"). Pixels are never scaled or
redrawn, and no generated art is used. Tiles by other artists in the Tome
pack (e.g. Henk Brouwer / Xerathul's Revenge) are not used.
