The sprites in the group folders here (Clergy, Demons, Mages, Nobles,
Orcs and Ogres, Rogues, Strangers, Townsfolk, Undead, Warriors) are cut by
tools/build_npc_sprites.py from the "Tome" tileset by David E. Gervais
(CC BY 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/,
http://pousse.rapiere.free.fr/tome/), already credited on the login screen:
AngbandTk dg_people32, dg_humans32, dg_classm32, dg_uniques32 and
dg_undead32, and Silmar beings / player. Changes: the background is made
transparent the same way as the older NPC art, and the odd-sized Silmar
sprites are set on a 32x32 cell. No other edits and no generated art.
